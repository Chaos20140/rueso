Aluminium elements from Salzkotten.

Since its founding in 1950, the name Rüther und Söhne has stood for high-quality building elements.

Today we are the partner for facades, windows and doors for architects, planners and public clients.

Our history↓ Careers at RÜSO
RÜSO GmbH

Initially producing plastic and aluminium elements, the company later switched to pure aluminium processing.

Today we are known and valued as a competent, reliable partner for facades, windows and doors in the Paderborn region and beyond.

Our clients are mainly architects, planners and public authorities.

Reliability, punctuality and flawless execution of all work are their most important criteria – and we align our thinking and actions accordingly.

In September 2021 the company was acquired by PLONKA GmbH. As part of an innovative group we leverage synergies and offer our clients even greater planning certainty.

Within the PLONKA Group, which specialises mainly in greenhouse construction, we cover facade construction for industry, commerce and public buildings.

Milestones

1950 to today.

1950 Founded by Franz Rüther Senior
1978 Renamed RÜSO Kunststoffverarbeitung GmbH & Co. KG – plastic and aluminium elements
1984 Taken over by Siegfried and Gerhard Rüther
1994 New administration building and production hall on the neighbouring site
2009 Aluminium construction spun off into the new RÜSO GmbH
2015 Michael Rüther joins the management
2021 Acquired by PLONKA GmbH – Michael Rüther remains managing director
2023 Headquarters relocated from Bad Wünnenberg to Salzkotten
